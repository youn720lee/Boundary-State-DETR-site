# Photograph used in Figures 1–3

The source photograph is **West Washington Market, Washington Street and Loew Avenue, Manhattan**, taken by Berenice Abbott on August 18, 1936. It belongs to the *Changing New York* collection at The New York Public Library, digital ID 482678. NYPL records the image as having no known copyright restrictions. [NYPL collection record](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/510d47d9-4ef3-a3d9-e040-e00a18064a99) · [NYPL image and rights record](https://www.flickr.com/photos/nypl/3110606120)

The figures use the 500 × 407 JPEG distributed in COCO val2017, image **567197**, license field 7. COCO annotation **397564** describes the foreground truck. Its bounding box is [290.04, 257.15, 159.27, 85.32] in absolute COCO xywh coordinates. [COCO image](https://s3.amazonaws.com/images.cocodataset.org/val2017/000000567197.jpg) · [COCO 2017 annotations](https://s3.amazonaws.com/images.cocodataset.org/annotations/annotations_trainval2017.zip)
