# COCO photographs in the paper figures

**Figures 1–6.** Berenice Abbott photographed *West Washington Market, Washington Street and Loew Avenue, Manhattan* on August 18, 1936. The New York Public Library identifies the work as part of its *Changing New York* collection, digital ID 482678, with no known copyright restrictions. COCO val2017 image 567197 includes the foreground truck (annotation 397564). [NYPL collection record](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/510d47d9-4ef3-a3d9-e040-e00a18064a99) · [Photograph and rights statement](https://www.flickr.com/photos/nypl/3110606120) · [COCO image](https://s3.amazonaws.com/images.cocodataset.org/val2017/000000567197.jpg)

**Figure 7.** The paper uses three COCO val2017 photographs to show selected detections before and after refinement. The source IDs and license entries below come from the [COCO 2017 annotations](https://s3.amazonaws.com/images.cocodataset.org/annotations/annotations_trainval2017.zip). The panels include annotated crops and candidate probability plots.
